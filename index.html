    Put it somewhere you think is a good halfway point, then search from there. The search checks both halves: from your position to the destination, and from the hall to your position.

    The sofa is modelled as solid blocks: a seat block up to arm height and a full-height backrest. Real cushions squash and legs unscrew, so a real sofa usually has a little more give than this.

    If a route is found, it exists for these exact numbers. If none is found, the search ran out of time; very tight fits can need a longer search.
